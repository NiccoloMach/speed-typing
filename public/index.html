How your score works

WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. Spaces count. Beginner passages use lowercase letters only. Accuracy is the percentage of characters in your current text that match the passage. Backspace is allowed, so corrections improve accuracy. The test ends when time runs out or you finish the passage. Paste is disabled for a fair practice session.
